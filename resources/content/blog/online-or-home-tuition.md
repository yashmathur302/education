Many parents ask us whether their child should study with a tutor at home or join live online classes. Both work well, and the right choice depends on your child, your schedule and the subject. Here is how to decide.

## When home tuition works best

A tutor who visits your home is a great fit for younger children and for students who need close guidance:

- The tutor can watch how your child writes and works through problems.
- Children who are easily distracted often focus better with someone beside them.
- Parents can meet the tutor regularly and see progress first-hand.

## When online classes work best

Live online classes suit older students and busy families:

- No travel time, so lessons fit easily around school and activities.
- Access to specialist tutors for subjects such as physics, accountancy or engineering courses.
- Sessions can often be recorded for revision later.

## Questions to ask before you choose

Think about how your child learns best. Do they ask questions freely on a video call, or do they go quiet? Do they have a quiet room and a reliable internet connection? Is the subject one that needs a lot of written practice that a tutor should check in person?

> There is no single right answer. The best option is the one your child will actually enjoy and stick with.

## Try before you decide

A demo class is the easiest way to find out. Many families start with one format and switch, or mix both: home tuition for a core subject and online classes for another. Tell us what your child needs and we will suggest the option that fits.
